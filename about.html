About

I'm a CS student in my 6th semester at Government Graduate College, Pasrur, Pakistan (2023–2027). I chose computer science to put math to work on real problems, which is what pulled me toward machine learning specifically — it's the field where getting the math wrong quietly produces a model that looks right and isn't.

Most of my project work has been built around one habit: never trust a validation score until you've checked what the split let the model see. That habit is the subject of the case studies on this site.

Outside of coursework, I read and build ML projects on my own — competitions, from-scratch implementations, and repos documenting what worked and what didn't.
